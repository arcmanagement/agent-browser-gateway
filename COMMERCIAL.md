## How to obtain a commercial license

Email **licensing@arcm.co.jp** with:

1. Your organization name and country of incorporation
2. A brief description of how you intend to use ABG (internal tool, embedded in product, hosted service, etc.)
3. Approximate scale (number of seats / end users / installations) if known
4. Any specific terms you need (term length, support, custom warranties, etc.)

We will respond within 5 business days with a quote and a draft license agreement.
