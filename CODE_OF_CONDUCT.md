# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) for issues, PRs, and any project-related space.

In short:

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Assume good faith. Most misunderstandings are misunderstandings.
- No harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

A private reporting channel will be published before the project is opened up for general use.
